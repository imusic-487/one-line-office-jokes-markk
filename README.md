## The entry

> I replied-all asking everyone to stop replying-all, and now there's a meeting on my calendar called 'Email Etiquette.'

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single line (118 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`. It checks first that the entry is one line of 1–199 characters with no leading or trailing whitespace, so the gate always passes.

The joke turns on a real office detail — the reply-all storm — and lands its laugh in the last few words, when the mess escalates into a calendar invite called "Email Etiquette."
